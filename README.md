# 🚀 Featured Project

## 🏔️ Yatri Setu — Himalayan Safety & Mobility Infrastructure

**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Project**

Yatri Setu is a digital infrastructure platform designed to improve **tourist safety, sustainable tourism, mobility and civic reporting** across Himalayan tourism and pilgrimage circuits.

🔗 **Repository:** [Yatri Setu](https://github.com/girisaurabh/yatri-setu)

🌐 **Live Demo:** [Open Yatri Setu](https://yatri-setu-az1f.vercel.app/)

### 💡 What It Solves

Himalayan tourist destinations can face problems such as:

* Tourist overcrowding
* Unsafe exploration near cliff areas
* Traffic bottlenecks
* Environmental degradation
* Difficulty discovering sustainable/offbeat destinations
* Inefficient civic issue reporting

Yatri Setu brings these problems together into a single digital platform.

### ✨ Key Features

#### 🚨 Safety & Hazard Detection

* 100m proximity hazard radar
* Static black-spot detection
* Visual safety alerts
* Offline browser-based emergency audio alert

#### 🗺️ Sustainable Tourism

* Offbeat destination discovery
* Tourist-load redistribution
* Elevation and crowd information
* Route navigation
* Community feedback and field reporting

#### 🚕 Mountain Mobility

* Valley bottleneck tracking
* Mountain transit information
* Capacity-aware mobility planning
* Regulated fare information

#### 🪪 Digital Yatri Pass

* Digital 24-hour traveller pass
* Offline-scannable QR token
* Checkpost and green-tax verification concept

#### 🧹 Civic Reporting

* Report waste and infrastructure issues
* GPS-linked incident information
* Civic sanitation reporting workflow

### 🛠️ Tech Used

`Next.js` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Leaflet` · `OpenStreetMap` · `Clerk` · `Web Audio API` · `QR`
